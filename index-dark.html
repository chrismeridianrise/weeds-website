The Band

A family trio rooted in tradition.

The Weeds are John, Tyler, and Evan Weed — a father and two sons from Carmel, California. They grew up traveling together to folk festivals and sessions across the West Coast, building a shared musical language across fiddle, harp, mandolin, guitar, tenor banjo, piano, and melodica.

Their music moves between traditional Irish tunes and original compositions, always with the same ease — as if there is no line between the two.

John Weed

Fiddle

The anchor of the trio. John's fiddle carries the Irish tradition with warmth and authority. He plays a violin by James Wimmer.

Evan Weed

Harp · Piano · Melodica

Evan's harp gives the trio its distinctive voice — resonant, unexpected, and grounding. He moves fluidly between harp, piano, and melodica.

Tyler Weed

Mandolin · Guitar · Banjo

Tyler drives the rhythm and melody in equal measure, moving across mandolin, guitar, and tenor banjo as the music demands.
