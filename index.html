Our Story

A musical conversation that begins and ends in ancient Ireland.

The Weeds are a dynamic trio rooted in traditions from Ireland and beyond. Hailing from the central coast of California, the Weeds consist of fiddler John Weed (Molly’s Revenge / New World String Project) and sons, Tyler Weed (mandolin, Irish tenor banjo, guitar) and Evan Weed (Celtic harp, piano, melodica).

Their music infuses the driving energy of a jam session with a unique contemporary flavor, seamlessly blending traditional melodies with innovative, original compositions and arrangements. Enjoy a musical journey that begins and ends in ancient Ireland, venturing ‘across the pond’ to explore influences from Appalachian mountain hollers to occasional hints of modern jazz.

The folk process unfolds as John enjoys a musical conversation with two of the next generation’s rising stars. Hearing sophisticated arrangements of driving Irish jigs, reels, and slower heartfelt selections, you’ll leave uplifted, knowing the future is in good hands as The Weeds raises your spirits, honoring this tradition with a vital, new voice.

The Band

A family trio rooted in tradition.

The Weeds are John, Tyler, and Evan Weed, a father and two sons from Carmel, California. They grew up traveling together to folk festivals and sessions across the West Coast, building a shared musical language across fiddle, harp, mandolin, guitar, tenor banjo, piano, and melodica.

Their music moves between traditional Irish tunes and original compositions, always with the same ease, as if there is no line between the two.

John Weed

Fiddle

The anchor of the trio, John's fiddle carries the Irish tradition with warmth and authority. He plays a violin by James Wimmer.

Evan Weed

Celtic Harp · Piano · Melodica

Evan's harp gives the trio its distinctive voice: resonant, unexpected, and grounding. He moves fluidly between harp, piano, and melodica.

Tyler Weed

Mandolin · Guitar · Tenor Banjo

Tyler drives the rhythm and melody in equal measure, moving across mandolin, guitar, and tenor banjo as the music demands.
